L70 — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

L70 is a personal, single-user application built and operated by its sole user. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With your explicit OAuth consent, L70 reads data from your WHOOP account: recovery, sleep, workouts, physiological cycles, basic profile, and body measurements. The app also stores nutrition and wellness logs that the user submits voluntarily via Telegram.

How data is stored

All data is stored in a private database on a private server controlled by the app owner. Access is restricted to the owner. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

What we do NOT do

  • No selling, sharing, or transferring data to third parties.
  • No advertising, tracking, or analytics.
  • No access by anyone other than the app owner (who is also the only user).

Data retention and deletion

Data is kept for personal health-tracking purposes. The owner can delete any or all data at any time. WHOOP access can be revoked at any time in the WHOOP app settings, which immediately stops all data collection.

Medical disclaimer

L70 is an informational tool only. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.
